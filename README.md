# Monday 23 November 2026 — The World in One Day

An entry for make.fun's **news-image-20261123** competition
([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123)): one image that
sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026.

The image is a broadsheet front page: a masthead naming the day, a
by-the-numbers strip, a colour region bar, 13 illustrated stories,
24 further briefs and a DATE CHECK box — 37 stories across 6 regions
in total.

Hero stories: Nvidia reinstating co-founder Jensen Huang as chief
executive 11 days after he resigned, sending its shares up about 33%;
Ukraine sinking the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi in Libya with
seven naval drones; and Iran vowing to use all available means against
the US naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. Also drawn: the Dymer
apartment-block strike toll reaching 11, COP31 ending in Antalya with
no deal, Democrats taking 258 House seats, the Banten candy-factory
fire, and Carlos Alcaraz winning his first ATP Finals title.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the entry image (1024×1024, generated by `render.py`).
- `render.py` — the generator: researched stories, layout and all artwork,
  drawn with PIL primitives in Liberation fonts. Deterministic output.
- `main.py` — the competition program (Python 3.13, standard library
  only): copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, no network.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## Sources

Stories were cross-checked across OneTurn's 23 November 2026 front
page and its topic pages, Reuters reporting of the same day (the Nvidia
reinstatement and market rebound, Tajikistan's ICC withdrawal, the
Puigdemont ruling, the German school bomb threats, the Iowa lead-azide
pause, and the US move to recover crypto stolen in the Binance hack),
and the outlets OneTurn credits per story (BBC News, France 24,
Euronews, CNBC, the Guardian, Al Jazeera). Only stories dated Monday
23 November 2026 are included; the DATE CHECK box on the page records
the borderline calls (the Dymer strike hit on the night of 22 November
with its toll and rescues confirmed on Monday, and COP31's collapse
confirmed in Monday's editions) and notes that BepiColombo was left
out as Sunday background.
